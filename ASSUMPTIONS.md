# Assumptions

| ID | Assumption | Why I needed it | Impact if it is wrong |
|---|---|---|---|
| AS-01 | The five Home Essentials answers can be mapped to bands (for example, postal code → rating zone, versioned in Salesforce), so each country has a finite answer space that can be rated in a daily batch | A pre-computed grid keeps quotes available while the org is down (≈ 97%, `SALESFORCE_CONSTRAINTS.md:15`), and this is needed for the 99.5% target (C1) | Without a grid, quotes use live rating, quote availability is limited by the org (≈ 97%), and [ADR-0002](docs/adr/ADR-0002-channel-to-core-integration.md) declares the gap with C1 |
| AS-02 | The Salesforce platform team can compute the grid inside each org after the 05:00 UTC publication and expose it through the System API in a few bulk calls, with a Q1 effort that fits next to configuring the product in three orgs. To be confirmed in a December spike, with an answer before 15 Dec 2026 | If the cell builds the grid with one REST call per combination, the 40 req/s limit and the daily budget restrict it (`SALESFORCE_CONSTRAINTS.md:19,31,35`) | Plan B: the cell builds the grid over REST with at most 10,000 combinations per country (10,000 ÷ 10 req/s ≈ 17 min; 10,000 ÷ 250,000 = 4% of the MX channel budget). If the answer space is larger, live rating becomes the main path |
| AS-03 | The data that issuance needs (country-specific documents and consents, `SALESFORCE_CONSTRAINTS.md:8`) can be validated through the System API before payment while the org is up, so issuance failures after payment are rare | Purchase in the same session (BR-01) charges the customer before the policy is issued | More cases reach the manual exception queue (refund or manual issuance), and the 3-minute issuance target is missed more often |
| AS-04 | Purchases are spread over time like quotes, so the share of purchases made while an org is down is close to its unavailability (≈ 3%, `SALESFORCE_CONSTRAINTS.md:15`). The material has no purchase or issuance data | To set the initial target for policies issued and sent in ≤ 3 min (95%, [`SLOS.md` §2](docs/operations/SLOS.md#2-slos)) | We recalibrate the target after 30 days of measured data |
| AS-05 | The Q3 synthetic availability of the quote journey (`DELIVERY_METRICS.md:17`) is a valid starting baseline for the quote targets of the current products, until the BFF SLI has 30 days of data | To set interim targets that the cell can meet, instead of a 99.5% that the Salesforce availability already rules out ([`SLOS.md` §2](docs/operations/SLOS.md#2-slos)) | The synthetic values are higher than the org's ≈ 97% (`SALESFORCE_CONSTRAINTS.md:15`), so the BFF, which also counts timeouts, may measure less. In that case the first recalibration lowers the targets to the measured baseline |
| AS-06 | Legal accepts a documented basis for regional services that process the personal data of the three countries, if each country's data and secrets stay in separate stores and people can access only the countries they serve. The two approved Azure regions (`SCENARIO.md:25`) can hold the data of the three countries | [ADR-0001](docs/adr/ADR-0001-regional-reuse-model.md) uses one regional deployment, and C5 asks for a documented basis | The components that process personal data (`channel-bff` and the quote Process API) move to one deployment per country (option B of ADR-0001). The code does not change |
| AS-07 | The MuleSoft deployment offers a persistent key-value store (Object Store) shared by the replicas of the Process API, with size and request-rate limits that fit the price grid and its daily load. We check it in the December spike, together with AS-02 | The grid must survive restarts and be readable by every replica ([`ARCHITECTURE.md` §4](docs/architecture/ARCHITECTURE.md#4-reaching-salesforce-c2-and-c6-at-month-end)), and the material does not say how MuleSoft is deployed (`SCENARIO.md:26`) | The grid moves to a small Azure Database for PostgreSQL owned by the Process API, a service already approved for the channel (`SCENARIO.md:25`). The Process API then needs network access to it, with a lookup p95 below 20 ms |
| AS-08 | The part of [TD-01, TD-03 and TD-04](context/TECH_DEBT_INVENTORY.md) that Home Essentials needs is 8–10 person-weeks, on top of the 45–55 of the build-once estimate: the consolidated Process API with country configuration (≈ 4), MX in `channel-bff` with `pg-channel-mx` (2–3) and the MX configuration of `web-quote-ui` (2–3) | The team says part of TD-01, TD-03 and TD-04 is needed first, but did not say which part or how much (`BUSINESS_REQUESTS.md:24`) | If it is already inside the 45–55, Q1 has ≈ 9 person-weeks more of margin. If it is larger, the Q1 gap grows and the MX plan B of 15 Mar applies ([`ROADMAP.md` §4](docs/roadmap/ROADMAP.md#4-plan-and-milestones)) |
| AS-09 | Home Essentials splits into ≈ 70% for the regional core plus MX, ≈ 15% for CO and ≈ 15% for CL; about 30% of the work is MuleSoft | To phase the work against the MX date and to size the load on the only MuleSoft developer (`TEAM.md:19`) | If the MuleSoft share is larger, the loan and the Nimbus help are not enough, and MX moves to 31 May |
| AS-10 | The 48 person-weeks of Nimbus per quarter include the run work of the MX legacy stack, about 20% as for the internal team, so ≈ 38 are available for planned work | `TEAM.md:18` gives Nimbus capacity without a run deduction, while the internal capacity has one (`TEAM.md:16`) | If Nimbus run work is larger, Nimbus contributes less to Home Essentials, and the Q1 gap is covered with the MX plan B |
| AS-11 | In December 2026 the cell can spend ≈ 5 person-weeks on the grid spike and the small P1 actions of INC-0907, as incident follow-up | The material gives capacity only for Q1 and Q2 2027 (`TEAM.md:13`), and the grid commitment and the MuleSoft loan are due by 15 Dec 2026 | The P1 actions move to January and compete with Home Essentials for the same capacity |
| AS-12 | Phase 1 of the assistant for MX customers (coverage answers, without pre-fill) costs ≈ 6–10 person-weeks after the pilot, and ≈ 2–3 for each additional country. Pre-fill (phase 2) is estimated at its own gate | To give the sponsor of BR-02 a date (`BUSINESS_REQUESTS.md:33`) | The Q4 2027 date for MX customers moves |
| AS-13 | The assistant costs USD 3,000–14,000 per year to run: ≈ 12,000 input and 1,200 output tokens per conversation; 18,000–100,000 conversations per month; ≈ USD 0.003–0.007 per conversation with the small model of the tenant service; USD 2,000–6,000 per year for the database and monitoring. Prices are orders of magnitude, and the spike measures the real ones | The material gives no LLM prices or usage, and the sponsor needs a cost range against the cap of USD 100,000 per year ([`FEASIBILITY_GENAI.md`](docs/roadmap/FEASIBILITY_GENAI.md)) | The large model is used less; if the cost still passes the cap, the go/no-go says no |
| AS-14 | The LLM service of the tenant can return answers that follow a schema, with a confidence score, and the approved Azure Database for PostgreSQL can store and search the document index (vector search) | Typed decisions and an index on an approved service keep cost and risk low ([`FEASIBILITY_GENAI.md` §2](docs/roadmap/FEASIBILITY_GENAI.md#2-options)) | Confidence comes from a second check, which costs more per conversation; the index needs a search service and a new Security approval |
| AS-15 | The cell's repositories are in Azure DevOps, and the MuleSoft applications can be deployed from Azure Pipelines (Mule Maven plugin or Anypoint CLI, with a connected app) | The pipeline template, the branch policies and the required-template check are Azure DevOps features. The material says only that internal components deploy through Azure Pipelines (`CURRENT_STATE.md:100`), not where the code is or how MuleSoft is deployed (`SCENARIO.md:26`) | The branch policies and the MuleSoft deploy step move to another tool; the gates stay the same |
| AS-16 | Both runtimes can split traffic between two versions: Azure Container Apps with revision weights, and the MuleSoft runtime or the API Manager for the Experience and Process APIs | The progressive traffic shift of [`SLOS.md` §5](docs/operations/SLOS.md#5-safe-deployment) | For MuleSoft, blue-green: the new version runs next to the old one, traffic moves at once, and the rollback moves it back |
| AS-17 | Security approves Azure App Configuration for the feature flags per country ([TD-10](context/TECH_DEBT_INVENTORY.md)), so a flag changes at runtime without a deployment | Release by country and the kill switch ([`SLOS.md` §5](docs/operations/SLOS.md#5-safe-deployment)). The service is not in the approved list (`SCENARIO.md:25`) | The flags live in a table of each country database (PostgreSQL, already approved), read with a short cache and changed through an audited admin endpoint |
| AS-18 | Load and fault-injection tests run against simulated System APIs with the Salesforce latency profile (rating p95 ≈ 1.1 s, `SALESFORCE_CONSTRAINTS.md:16`), plus a short test against a Salesforce sandbox per country. They never use the production allocation | The production orgs are shared with Payments, and INC-0907 grew from Salesforce consumption | We agree test windows and sandbox limits with the Salesforce platform team |
| AS-19 | The MX delivery metrics come from Nimbus reports (`DELIVERY_METRICS.md:3`) and are not fully comparable with the pipeline data of CO and CL | To set the delivery targets of the MX legacy components ([`ENGINEERING_STANDARDS.md` §5](docs/engineering/ENGINEERING_STANDARDS.md#5-delivery-targets-for-the-end-of-q2-2027)) | We recalibrate the MX targets after two months of Meridian pipeline data |
| AS-20 | The five Home Essentials questions are the postal code, the dwelling type, owner or tenant, a built-area band and a contents-value band | The contract needs typed answers ([`quote-experience-api.yaml`](docs/api/quote-experience-api.yaml)), and the material does not list the questions (`BUSINESS_REQUESTS.md:10`). Bands keep the grid finite (AS-01) and free text out of the request | The Product Owner changes the fields of `HomeEssentialsAnswers` before the MX UAT gate. The rest of the contract and the grid model stay the same |
| AS-21 | The Security office accepts the use of the tenant LLM service by the cell's developers, Nimbus included, as internal use under the existing approval, with an IDE plug-in that only calls the tenant endpoint. Prompts stay in the tenant and are not used for training. The service may offer a filter for protected material | `BUSINESS_REQUESTS.md:47` approves the service "for internal pilots only", and the guidelines allow no other tool ([`AI_GUIDELINES.md` §1](docs/ai/AI_GUIDELINES.md#1-allowed-tools)) | We ask Security to extend the approval; until then the cell uses no AI tools. Without the filter, the license gate and the review rule of [`AI_GUIDELINES.md` §4](docs/ai/AI_GUIDELINES.md#4-accountability-security-and-intellectual-property) remain |
